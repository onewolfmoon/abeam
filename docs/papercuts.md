AirPlay and Google Cast papercuts
=================================

**Author:** Wesley

Here's a list of the problems I ran into trying to use an Apple TV or a Chromecast to watch video and mirror my screen.

It's my goal to have Abeam work better than this baseline.

AirPlay
-------

- I can’t use other apps while I AirPlay a movie. AirPlay aggessively replaces the playing video with any other video open on my phone like social media content or video ads.
- I have to max out my phone’s volume to watch Twitch over AirPlay. Twitch ties the AirPlay volume to the media volume on the phone. This also means that the phone’s on max volume the next time I head out.
- I can’t follow along with the The New York Times Crossword over AirPlay screen mirroring. This crossword is one of the kinds of content that causes heavy artifacting when using AirPlay screen mirroring.
- I can’t listen to music using AirPlay screen mirroring because it doesn’t include audio.

Google Cast
-----------

- I don’t want to find or use a remote control when I’m casting from my phone. When I cast a YouTube video to a device with a remote, I often have to dismiss or confirm some overlay with the remote control.
- I can’t watch many YouTube videos on classic Chromecasts. Some videos silently fail to play. Others fail to play with a message about the account being in “Restricted Mode”.
- I can’t listen to music using Chromecast screen casting because it doesn’t include audio.

Both
----

- I use apps that don’t show an AirPlay or Google Cast button. If the app publisher doesn’t implement it, I’m out of luck.
- When something doesn’t quite work, I need one or more of Apple, Google, and an app developer to help. By basing Abeam on open source software running on a desktop operating system, improvements are no longer out of reach.
