# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy was last updated on August 29, 2026. Updates to this privacy policy will be posted here.

Abeam and Abaft do not collect, store, or share information about how you use them.

If you consent to automatic update checks, Abeam Receiver contacts GitHub once a day to retrieve update information. No information about how you use Abeam Receiver is sent as part of this check.

If you download Abeam or Abaft from the Apple App Store, that’s covered under any agreements you have with Apple about your use of the App Store. If you use Abeam or Abaft on iOS or macOS, that’s covered any agreements you have with Apple about your use of iOS or macOS.
